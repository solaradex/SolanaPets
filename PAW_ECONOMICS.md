# $PAW Token Economics

**The one rule: $PAW is earned, never given.** No airdrops. No freebies.
You get $PAW by taking care of your pet, completing quests, and winning shows.
That's it.

## The face of $PAW: Luna 🌙

**Luna** — Genesis Founder #1, the Moonlit Otter — is the face of the $PAW
token. Her crescent moon marking is the $PAW logo. Token branding, imagery,
and metadata all feature Luna. When you see the moon, you think $PAW.

## What $PAW is for

- **Marketplace** — buy pets, accessories, habitat upgrades
- **Breeding fees** — pay to breed your pets
- **Trading** — $PAW will be listed on Solana DEXs so it has real market value

## How you earn it

| Action | $PAW earned |
|---|---|
| Feed / play / clean / rest / show affection | 1 $PAW each (base) |
| Bond level bonus | Up to 3x (the more bonded your pet, the more you earn) |
| Daily quest | 10 $PAW bonus |
| Monthly pet show win | 25–100 $PAW |

**Bond levels:** every 1,000 bond XP = +1 level. Each level adds 10% to your
earnings, up to 3x at level 20. A well-loved pet literally earns more.

## The limits (why $PAW won't inflate to zero)

1. **50 $PAW per pet per day, max.** No matter how much you play.
2. **1 hour between claims.** No spam-clicking for tokens.
3. **1 billion $PAW total, ever.** Hard-coded into the program. When it's
   gone, it's gone — no one can mint more, not even us.
4. **Burning.** Every time you spend $PAW (marketplace, breeding, accessories),
   those tokens are *destroyed* — gone forever. The more the game is used,
   the scarcer $PAW gets.

## The math

- Year one (15,000 Genesis pets, realistic play): ~55M $PAW minted — **under 6% of supply**
- Full scale (750,000 pets): the 1B cap is the circuit breaker, and burn sinks
  push back against it indefinitely
- Healthy game economies (like Axie's early days) work because earning is
  *slow* and spending is *constant*. We built it that way on purpose.

## What this means for you

If you take great care of your pets, you'll earn steady $PAW. If you want
more, you can buy it on a DEX from other players. Either way, the token has
real value because it's backed by real gameplay — not printed out of thin air.
