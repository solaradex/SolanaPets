# SolanaPets — The 8 Creator Pets (Canonical Reference)

> **Locked in by Brad, October 13, 2026.** This is the canon. Every trait below
> is fully visual on the founder pet and seeds the gene pool that all future
> pets carry. See `COLOR_POOLS.md` for the base coat/eye gene pools these
> traits layer on top of.

## The rule

Each creator pet carries **2 legendary traits** + **1 mythic mutation**, all
fully visual. This combination is what makes a founder a founder — and it's the
genetic seed of the entire SolanaPets ecosystem.

- **Legendary** = one striking feature. Glowing eyes, a single luminescent
  marking, a shimmer in the coat. It makes you stop scrolling.
- **Mythic** = transformative and unmistakable. A full-body aura, star patterns
  in the fur, a coat that shifts color. It makes people gasp from across the room.

## Gene pool model

EVERY pet — not just the founders — carries the full mythic/legendary gene set
in its DNA. But most will never visually express them. Expression is rare
(low-penetrance model): only a small percent of pets will ever show a legendary
or mythic trait visibly. The founders show everything because they ARE the gene
pool. A bred pet showing a mythic trait means the genes woke up — and that's
what makes it special.

express moon-markings or glowing tails for generations.

## On-chain mapping

These traits are encoded in the pet registry program (`programs/pet-registry`).
See `LegendaryTrait` and `MythicMutation` enums in `src/lib.rs`, and
`creator_pet_traits()` for the canonical ID → traits mapping used by the
future `mint_creator_pet` instruction. IDs 1–8 can never be minted through
`mint_nfp` — they are permanently reserved for these eight founders.
